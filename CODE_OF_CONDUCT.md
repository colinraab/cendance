# Code of Conduct

cendance is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free project
community. Participate with empathy, assume good intent while remaining open
to correction, and focus criticism on ideas and work rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted
sexual attention, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private
information, or retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, issues, or
other participation that violates these expectations. They may warn,
temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participants when necessary to
protect the community.

Report sensitive conduct concerns privately through the maintainer contact
options on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/colinraab). Do not expose
private details in a public issue. Reports will be handled as confidentially as
practical, with fairness to everyone involved.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone is representing the
project in public.
